## 3) 18th-Century Story (Wikipedia + Rijksmuseum provenance)

Painted 1642, commissioned c. 1639 for the Kloveniersdoelen great hall; paid 1,600 guilders. In 1715 moved to Amsterdam Town Hall and trimmed on all four sides (73 years after completion), losing two left-side figures, the arch top, balustrade and step edge; missing portions not found. A shield with 18 names was painted c. 1715. Web additions: dark varnish caused the 'night' misnomer; WWII removal and Maastricht cave storage 1939-43; 1975 knife attack and 1990 acid attack; Operation Night Watch (2019+); 44.8-gigapixel image (2020); 2021 AI reconstruction of trimmed edges from Gerrit Lundens' copy; the dog modelled after an Adriaen van de Venne illustration.
